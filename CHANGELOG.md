- Paper-test seat relay (`league-relay`, library and binary), for games 3 onward.
  - Reads JSON seat files (`openrouter` with case-insensitively unique `[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9_-]*` guild names, pinned `provider/model` slugs, temperature, `max_output_tokens`, per-seat wall timeout, and `max_cost_per_call`; `file` seats report manual relay and make no call).
  - `league-relay turn` prepends `--wrapper` on a seat's first turn, replays `relay/seats/<Guild>.jsonl` (`user` and `assistant` turns only, no system prompt), dispatches seats concurrently with isolated failures, writes replies byte-identically to `from-seat/day-DD-<round>-<Guild>.md`, and logs every attempt to `relay/calls.jsonl`.
  - Delivery failures (transport error, HTTP error, empty reply, or wall timeout) re-send the identical request body once (`harness`), then log `NO_REPLY` with no `from-seat` file; replies arriving after the timeout log `LATE_DISCARDED` with cost and tokens before any re-send; wrong-form non-empty replies are not retried.
  - Live calls require `--live`, `--cap-credits <n>`, and `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` through `LiveGate`. `--game-dir` must already exist before anything is created (`exit 1` otherwise). Each `run_turn` holds an exclusive OS file lock on `relay/turn.lock` before any ledger read or cap reservation and verifies `relay/turn.lock` file identity across reservations, ledger/side-file writes, and turn finalization (`TurnLocked` exits `4`, refusing concurrent turns before any transport call, or halting further calls while recording already-spent charges if `relay/turn.lock` is deleted or replaced mid-turn). Before each call goes out, `league-relay` checks `--cap-credits` under one lock (`charged + reserved_in_flight + max_cost_per_call <= cap`, `CAP_REACHED` otherwise) and atomically persists a `RESERVED` entry tagged with a unique `reservation_id` at `max_cost_per_call` to `relay/calls.reserved.jsonl` (serialized with `relay/calls.pending.jsonl` under `relay/calls.side.lock`), removing the transient reservation only after the call's final line (carrying that `reservation_id`) has been `sync_all`-persisted so normal runs leave strictly one line per call in `relay/calls.jsonl` without a power-loss gap, or reconciling an orphaned `RESERVED` line idempotently by `reservation_id` into `relay/calls.jsonl` after a crash or exit `3`. Sent attempts without a valid `usage.cost` (including timeouts and usage-less replies) charge `max_cost_per_call` in `charged_credits`; failed appends to `relay/calls.jsonl` hold the charge in memory, stop the turn (`LEDGER_ERROR`), and immediately write `relay/calls.pending.jsonl` atomically under `relay/calls.side.lock` via `calls.pending.jsonl.tmp` + `sync_all` + rename (retrying `relay/calls.jsonl` at turn end, migrating side-file lines once across runs, or keeping the `max_cost_per_call` reservation in `relay/calls.reserved.jsonl`, printing all old and new held lines to `stderr` with guidance not to hand-enter the kept reservation, and exiting `3` on `UnrecordedCharge` if neither file can be written); missing completion cost (`COST_UNKNOWN`) or cost above `max_cost_per_call` (`COST_OVER_ESTIMATE`) fails closed for further calls in that turn.
  - `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` is read from the environment only and redacted (capping error bodies at `4 KiB`, scrubbing `8+` character substrings before truncation, and gating test-only mock panic injection out of release builds) across `Debug`, errors, binary `stderr` panic hook, CLI rejections, and call logs.

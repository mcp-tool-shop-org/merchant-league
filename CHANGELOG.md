## [Unreleased]

- State predicates, the approved Step 1 of the hint design (`league-core`, no change to anything a seat is shown):
  - **Five predicates** in the new `league-core::predicates` module, each a pure function of the engine state's public parts plus the asking seat's own holdings: `upkeep_and_debt`, `spoilage_and_conversion`, `scarcity_and_tariffs`, `coalition_reach` / `all_coalitions_reach` and `season_end_and_marks`, gathered by `compute_seat_state_predicates` into one serialisable `SeatStatePredicates` struct (`STATE_PREDICATES_VERSION = 1`). Their numbers come from the engine's own rule code, now shared rather than copied: the Sweep's yield, decay-rate, upkeep-settlement, input-clamp and tariff helpers in `engine.rs`, and the close mark, whose body moved into `close_mark_breakdown` so `mark_good_holding_at_close` returns the same value and the predicates can also count the units marked at the `50%` floor. No predicate reads another seat's mandate, any sealed or unrevealed turn orders, unrealized drawn shocks or another seat's free balances.
  - **Replay record section:** `replay_season_with_state_predicates` and `attach_state_predicates` replay a log and add an optional `StatePredicateSection` (every seat's predicates at the start of every turn, each Sweep's per-seat shortfall, debt and spoilage as the engine reported them, and every seat's predicates at the close). `ReplayRecord` writes the log's own keys first and the section as one extra key only when present, so a record without it is byte-identical to the bare `SeasonReplayLog`; `replay_season` itself is unchanged, and every existing state digest and golden test is unchanged.
  - **Paper-test metrics** from the section: `held_enough_but_short_turns`, `covered_with_own_offers_but_short_turns`, `spoiled_units_over_season`, `stranded_stock_at_close` and `compute_season_metrics`.
  - Tests in `crates/league-core/tests/state_predicates.rs`: a hand-worked case per predicate, agreement with what the Sweep then does (shortfall debt, spoilage and the close mark), a privacy test per predicate, determinism across `N = 4, 5, 6`, and the replay section on and off against the rich-season golden digest.
  - **Review follow-ups (fix 1):** the privacy test now runs the close rule's bid walk against bids from the varied seats and varies only what stays private under the 2026-11-16 ruling (mandate cards and commitments, unrevealed commitments and sealed bundles, unpublished drawn shocks) on seats 1, 2 and 3; cartel-domain bids are tested for executability under the close rule (a bid the seat cannot sell into changes none of its predicates; a member, a broker seller or a hybrid bid from a broker maker does change its mark); independent close-mark checks, positive project batch counts and a converter run checked against the Sweep. The shortfall metric is renamed `held_enough_but_short_turns` and joined by `covered_with_own_offers_but_short_turns` (predicate 1 gains `held_on_own_resting_sells`). `settle_upkeep` reads the parity only on a shortfall again and `batches_covered_by_inputs` uses a checked read, so truncated states do not panic. `ReplayRecord::from_json_slice` refuses a section whose `table_id` is not the log's.
  - **`deterministic_full_seasons_4_5_6_seats_and_json_replay_match_bit_for_bit` fixed:** it drew fresh OS entropy for the mandate deal and VRF seed, and its standings assertion expected exact `total_equity` ties ordered by seat, while since PR #40 the engine breaks them by the closing beacon's `standings_tie_key`. It now uses fixed fixture entropy and expects the beacon tie-break; the determinism and JSON replay assertions are unchanged.

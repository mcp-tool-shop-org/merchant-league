## 0. Scope, Premise Corrections, and Epistemic Rules

Under the `2026-11-17` scope ruling recorded in `#68` and [`docs/design-v2.md`](design-v2.md#L5) and the `2026-11-17` rulings on §6, `v0.1.0` is **Club Mode** (`TableMode::Club`)—an offline terminal season in `league-tui` over pure-Rust `league-core` with humans on one machine and simple rule-based bot seats, playing a single-human season (`H = 1`) in roughly `90 minutes` `[target]` at a `210 s` default turn clock (`180–300 s` configurable; multi-human tables add hand-off grace per §1.2)—and every `2026-11-17` design-review improvement tracked in `#68` lands before `1.0` in an order paced by the Publisher.

Three rules govern every figure and citation in this document:

1. **No simulated or newly computed figures:** Every quantitative value either cites its source (`path:line` at `ed872e53bc9381ac2e2f76167164173b113788f2`, arithmetic directly derived from those turn bounds, or a named report file) or is explicitly marked `[target]`.
2. **Unchecked external citations:** External academic or industry studies referenced through deep-research reports (seeds `25–33`) have **not** been audited at primary source here and are marked `(unchecked at source, via <report>)` whenever a design choice relies on them.
3. **Four premise corrections applied (`2026-11-17-rnd-premise-check-ml-design-advice.md` & addendum):**
   - *Preview built strictly on `league-core` simulation, never predicates:* `crates/league-core/src/predicates.rs:1-20` (`compute_seat_state_predicates` at `predicates.rs:625-642`) computes static start-of-turn diagnostics (*if nothing changes before Sweep*). At baseline `ed872e53` that path sat behind `TurnEngine::preview_bundle*`; after Wave 2 Card 2 / 2b, the interactive human What-If Sandbox builds strictly on [`preview_what_if`](../crates/league-core/src/observation.rs) (`SweepMode::PreviewSteps0To7` over `PublicPreviewState`) with caller-chosen counterparty assumptions (plus pre/post `SeatStatePredicates` gauges), and the optional AI-seat preview in arm `v2_preview_2026_11_20_r2` (updating historical Card 7 `v2_preview_2026_11_17_r1`) builds strictly on [`preview_seat_bundle`](../crates/league-core/src/observation.rs) (`SweepMode::PreviewSteps0To7`, seat's own orders only, empty peers, neutral beacon), never a second rules engine.
   - *Currency codes already draw from a 61-word English glossary:* [`ALLOWED_COMMODITY_GLOSSARY`](../crates/league-gen/src/schema.rs#L540-L602) already restricts generated 3-letter codes to `61` curated trade nouns (`ALM` = `"alum"`, `TMB` = `"timber"`) while rejecting uncurated strings such as `QZX` (`schema.rs:688-706`) and excluding `511` entries in [`DENIED_CURRENCY_CODES`](../crates/league-gen/src/schema.rs#L624-L677). Each table samples `2 * N` distinct codes (`8`, `10`, or `12` goods for `N in 4..=6` seats at `crates/league-gen/src/generator.rs:452, 563-570`) rather than a single paired raw/refined chain.
   - *Variable season horizon:* Seasons run `24..=32` turns (`CLOSING_ROLL_START_TURN = 24`, `HARD_TURN_CAP = 32`, `DEFAULT_CLOSING_HAZARD_BPS = [3_500; 8]`, expected length `25.80` turns and `3.19%` Turn-32 probability per [`types.rs`](../crates/league-core/src/types.rs#L62-L75) and [`docs/design-v2.md`](design-v2.md#L242-L245)). Expected duration therefore depends on the `210 s` default clock plus hot-seat hand-off grace across `H` human seats (§1.2).
   - *Benchmark comparability for AI preview:* Granting AI seats exact pre-commit simulation alters what automated Evaluation Mode measures (compressing arithmetic-error variance and shifting invalid-transaction / stability metrics in [`docs/design-v2.md`](design-v2.md#L310)), so AI preview access sits off the `v0.1.0` critical path (Card 7) behind `--preview-tool <off|on>` (`off` default) and named preview arm `v2_preview_2026_11_20_r2` (`v2_preview_2026_11_17_r1` retained sealed).

### 1.2 Lobby Bootstrap, `TableMode::Club`, Turn Clock Split, and Regent Doctrine

Launching the `league-tui` binary opens `crossterm` raw mode and `EnterAlternateScreen`—only **after** verifying an attached interactive TTY (`TTY_REQUIRED` exits `1` beforehand, checked by `league_tui::tests::cli_exit_codes_cover_0_1_2_3_and_non_tty_exits_1_before_raw_mode`) and installing a terminal-restoring panic hook (`league_tui::tests::panic_hook_restores_terminal_prints_one_redacted_line_and_gates_backtrace_on_debug`). From the interactive Lobby screen ([`render_lobby_screen`](../crates/league-seat-ui/src/lib.rs) / [`LobbyHostSession`](../crates/league-tui/src/host.rs)), players configure:

- **Table composition (`N in 4..=6`):** Seat count `4`, `5`, or `6` (`TableSpec::validate` at [`types.rs`](../crates/league-core/src/types.rs#L616-L622)), assigning each seat `0..N` (`H in 1..=N` human seats; zero humans is refused with `NO_HUMAN_SEATS`) to either `Human` (local hot-seat player, optional 4-digit table-local PIN) or `ClubBot` (deterministic rules-only opponent tagged `SeatIdentity::AiSyndicate` at [`types.rs`](../crates/league-core/src/types.rs#L411-L416), preserving upfront AI disclosure from [`docs/design-v2.md`](design-v2.md#L270), verified by `league_tui::host::tests::lobby_refuses_every_invalid_seed_seat_and_clock_input_by_name_and_holds_no_private_state` and `league_tui::tests::lobby_loop_configures_table_starts_behind_curtain_and_quits_cleanly`).
- **Standing Regent doctrine per human seat (fixes `regent == None` silent no-op; public on purpose):** Because `step1_validate_reveals_and_regents` (`engine.rs:2113-2119`) and `evaluate_c1a_idle_takeover` (`engine.rs:3348`) execute `synthesize_regent_bundle` **only** when `spec.seats[idx].regent` is `Some(doctrine)`, the Lobby initializes every `Human` seat with [`DEFAULT_CLUB_HUMAN_REGENT_DOCTRINE`](../crates/league-core/src/club/host.rs#L54) (`risk_tolerance_bps: 5_000`, `min_raw_reserve: UNIT_SCALE`, `auto_cash_due_claims: true`), verified by `lobby_refuses_every_invalid_seed_seat_and_clock_input_by_name_and_holds_no_private_state` (in-UI Regent customization remains a post-Card-5 option, while `ClubBot` seats keep `regent: None`). **`SeatConfig::regent` is intentionally public table configuration (`C4`):** `PublicTableSpec.seats[*].regent` exposes every seat's `Option<RegentDoctrine>` to every seat in `SeatObservation` so all seats know upfront which seats carry Standing Regent fallback settings. On timed timeout, seats with `Some(doctrine)` receive automatic custodial execution via `synthesize_regent_bundle`; seats whose human player opted out (`regent: None`) have no Regent action synthesized on timeout (`SealedOrderBundle::default()` settles and `consecutive_misses` increments), and either setting converts `Human -> AiSyndicate` after `IDLE_MISS_TAKEOVER_TURNS = 3` (`types.rs:129`, `engine.rs:2120-2125, 3342-3375`).
- **Seed selection strictly in `SeedPool::Dev` (`0..=9_999`):** Calling `is_reserved_test_seed(seed)` ([`schema.rs`](../crates/league-gen/src/schema.rs#L146-L155)) and `generate_ring_table_for_setup(seed, TableSetupOptions { purpose: TablePurpose::Dev, training_logged: false }, &config)` ([`generator.rs`](../crates/league-gen/src/generator.rs#L237-L263)). Any malformed, `train` (`10_000..=999_999`), `probe` (`1_000_000..=1_099_999`), `held_out` (`2_000_000..=2_099_999`), `sealed` (`>= 2^63`), or `unassigned` seed (`schema.rs:34-71, 95-101`) is refused by name (`INVALID_SEED_SYNTAX` / `RESERVED_SEED_FORBIDDEN` / `SEED_POOL_MISMATCH`), verified by `lobby_refuses_every_invalid_seed_seat_and_clock_input_by_name_and_holds_no_private_state`.
- **Distinct `TableMode::Club` (`2026-11-17` Director Decision Q2):** Offline Club tables run under **`TableMode::Club`** ([`TableMode`](../crates/league-core/src/types.rs#L542-L553), landed in Card 2)—**never** reusing `TableMode::Eval`—so offline human games can never enter automated Evaluation datasets (**stated requirement on future `Eval` dataset loaders:** no automated dataset loader exists in the workspace yet, and every future `Eval` loader and aggregator must require `log.mode == TableMode::Eval`, enforced in `league-core` by `SeasonReplayLog::from_eval_json_slice`, `ReplayRecord::from_eval_json_slice`, and `replay_eval_season`, rejecting `TableMode::Club` and `TableMode::Game`; generic `from_json_slice` / `replay_season` audit helpers still accept Club logs and must not be used by Eval dataset pipelines). For turn beacons and replay verification, `TableMode::Club` uses OS-CSPRNG [`SealedEvalSeed`](../crates/league-core/src/commit.rs) and **`ReplayBeaconMode::EvalVrf`**, verifying 80-byte `c2sp.org/vrf-r255` proofs against `eval_vrf_pubkey`, while `TurnEngine::sweep_turn_with_eval_vrf_detailed`, `SeasonReplayLog::build_checked`, and `replay_season_inner` pair `TableMode::Club` strictly with `ReplayBeaconMode::EvalVrf` and clear `SeedPool::Dev` (`0..=9_999`, `!is_reserved_test_seed`) seeds. Turn-1 mandates deal via OS-CSPRNG `deal_graded_mandates` ([`commit.rs`](../crates/league-core/src/commit.rs#L628-L640)). Neither `SealedEvalSeed` nor `SealedMandateDeal` ever derives from the layout `seed`.
- **Turn clock split, CLI overrides & recomputed session pacing (`2026-11-17` Director Decision Q3):** Offline Club Mode defaults to **`210 s` (`3m30s`) whole-turn budget**, configurable across **`180 s..=300 s`** (outside bounds refused with `INVALID_TURN_CLOCK`), with early commit advance when all `H` human seats lock and an **Untimed Hot-Seat** option (public-Testnet Game Mode keeps `10-minute` Live Club turns at [`docs/design-v2.md`](design-v2.md#L263)). CLI flags (`--turn-clock <180..=300>`, `--untimed`, `--pins`, `--save-dir <DIR>`, `--resume <PATH>`, `--error-format <text|json>`, `--log-level <silent|normal|verbose|debug>`, `-q`, `-v`, `--debug`, `--log-file <PATH>`) override conflicting Lobby and saved `.seal` choices (documented in `--help` and checked by `league_tui::tests::cli_args_parse_and_dispatch_all_paths` and `league_tui::host::tests::lobby_built_table_equals_equivalent_cli_flag_table_and_cli_overrides_win`); valid Lobby startup opens Seat `0`'s initial Curtain cleanly via `open_curtain_for_seat` with zero `SeatObservation`s built beforehand. Within each timed turn of total budget `B` seconds across `H` human seats (`75%` Pass A / `25%` Pass B matching `DEFAULT_CANCEL_LOCK_BPS = 2_500`, `types.rs:149`), each human seat receives `floor(0.75 * B / H)` seconds in Pass A and `floor(0.25 * B / H)` seconds in Pass B before auto-lock/Regent timeout (`157 s / 52 s` at `H = 1`; `39 s / 13 s` at `H = 4`; `26 s / 8 s` at `H = 6` under default `B = 210 s`; idle `SubPassB` timeout omits un-staged reveals so `TurnEngine::step1_validate_reveals_and_regents` invokes `synthesize_regent_bundle`, verified by `league_tui::tests::fake_clock_poll_loop_updates_countdown_expires_pass_to_clean_curtain_regent_acts_and_untimed_never_expires`). Resuming a saved game restarts the active human seat's pass clock at its full per-seat pass budget rather than preserving mid-pass elapsed seconds. Curtain transitions pause the active seat clock up to a `5 s` `[target]` grace cap per hand-off (`2 * H` transitions when `H >= 2`, `0 s` when `H = 1`). Across `24..=32` turns (`E[T] = 25.80`, `3.19%` Turn-32 probability, `types.rs:63-75`, `docs/design-v2.md:244`):
  - At strict `210 s/turn` (`H = 1`, or zero hand-off grace before early locks): `84.0 min` (`24` turns), **`90.3 min` (`E[T] = 25.80` turns)**, `112.0 min` (`Turn 32`).
  - At `H = 4` taking the full `5 s` `[target]` grace on all `8` hand-offs (`250 s/turn` ceiling before early locks): `100.0 min` (`24` turns), **`107.5 min` (`25.80` turns)**, `133.3 min` (`Turn 32`); at `B = 300 s` (`5 min`, allocating `56 s` Pass A / `18 s` Pass B at `H = 4`), un-accelerated seasons span `120.0–160.0 min` (`129.0 min` at `25.80` turns) plus hand-off grace unless seats advance early.

### 1.6 Save / Resume Threat Model and Secret Isolation

- **Threat model (`B4` settled choice):** Offline Club Mode save/resume guarantees **accidental-exposure screen privacy among honest hot-seat players sharing one terminal**; it does **not** defend against a local OS user with shell access to the same user account reading local process memory or host files.
- **No raw VRF seed on disk, and the 32 pre-derived VRF proofs live exclusively in `.seal` (`B4`):** Because `eval_vrf_alpha(table_id, Turn(t))` (`commit.rs:1333-1339`) depends solely on `(table_id, t)` for `t in 1..=HARD_TURN_CAP` (`32`), Lobby setup generates an ephemeral `SealedEvalSeed`, records `eval_vrf_pubkey`, pre-derives all `32` 80-byte `eval_vrf_proof`s (`2,560` bytes) upfront via `prove_turn`, and drops `SealedEvalSeed` in memory before Turn 1 starts. **Neither file ever writes a raw 32-byte `SealedEvalSeed` to disk.** However, because `verify_and_extract_eval_vrf_beacon` derives every turn's `turn_beacon` (`beta[..32]`) deterministically from `(eval_vrf_pubkey, proof_t)` and `draw_shocks_for_turn` / `close_roll_bps_from_beacon` depend solely on `turn_beacon`, **all `32` pre-derived `eval_vrf_proof`s are just as sensitive as `SealedEvalSeed` itself**—anyone holding them with `eval_vrf_pubkey` can compute every future beacon, every future shock draw, and every closing roll. Accordingly, the `32` pre-derived proofs go **strictly out of `.save.json`** and reside solely inside `.seal`.
- **What each file holds and who may open it in hot-seat play (`B4`, `C2`, Card 5 Part B2a):**
  - **`<save-dir>/club-<table_id>.save.json` (public save file — safe for any seat or spectator to open):** Stores strictly public information: `RingTable` (clear `dev` seed), sanitized public table state matching the public `SeatObservation` projection (realized `active_shocks` and already-announced `public_forecasts` with `forecast_turn <= last_swept_turn` and zero magnitude—**never** raw `TurnEngine`/`EngineState`, **never** unannounced `drawn_shocks`, **never** `mandate_commits`/`mandate_reveals` or `dealt_mandate_commits`, **never** `turn_commits`/`turn_reveals`, **never** `commit_salt_entropy`/`current_turn_salts`, **never** uncommitted staged bundles, private notes, or 4-digit PINs, **never** `last_turn_beacon`/`last_closing_roll_bps`, and **never** duplicate receipt histories, holding only one `Vec<PublicSweepReceipt>` history), and display preferences (`mid_turn_save_and_resume_at_curtains_matches_uninterrupted_receipts_keeps_secrets_in_seal_and_wipes_seal`, `commit_salts_draw_from_sealed_entropy_differ_across_identical_public_tables_stay_out_of_save_json_and_replay_verifies`). **Unannounced shocks NEVER appear in `.save.json`.**
  - **`<save-dir>/club-<table_id>.seal` (private local-trust sidecar — NO player may open during an active season):** Stores all secret and hidden-state material needed to resume mid-turn and export: the `32` pre-derived `eval_vrf_proof`s (`t in 1..=32`), hidden `drawn_shocks`, `dealt_mandate_commits`, `SealedMandateDeal` (`MandateReveal`s), completed `Vec<LoggedTurn>`, live `live_state_digests`, OS-CSPRNG `commit_salt_entropy` and per-turn `current_turn_salts` (drawn via `commit::draw_nonzero_256_bits` and refreshed per Sweep), plus [`ClubPauseCursor`](../crates/league-core/src/club/host.rs) and [`ClubSessionCheckpoint`](../crates/league-core/src/club/host.rs) (`pending_bundles`, `sub_pass_b_committed`, `seat_notes`, `seat_pins`, `pins_enabled`, `turn_clock_secs`, `untimed`). Hand-written redacting `fmt::Debug` across all private seat, view, view-building-block, UI, checkpoint, and host types (`SealedOrderBundle`, `PrivateMandate`, `PreimageSecret`, `BundleDraftAction`, `SeatIntent`, `SeatObservation`, `CounterpartyAssumptions`, `HumanWhatIfRequest`, `AiPreviewRequest`, `WhatIfPreview`, `SeatScreenView`, `ViewPanel`, `ViewZonePanel`, `ViewRow`, `ViewSpan`, `ViewLabel`, `ViewModal`, `ViewStatusLine`, `ViewTable`, `ViewTableColumn`, `ViewTableRow`, `ViewTableCell`, `ViewTabBar`, `ViewTabItem`, `MarketComposerFields`, `BundleComposerFields`, `SeatUiState`, `SeatUiStep`, `ClubSessionCheckpoint`, `ClubHost`, `TuiHostSession`, `CurtainPinEntry`, `TuiError`) prevents secret leakage via `{:?}`/`{:#?}` (`debug_and_pretty_debug_redact_all_private_seat_types`, `debug_and_pretty_debug_redact_seat_ui_state_and_composers`, `debug_and_pretty_debug_redact_club_checkpoint_mandate_and_reveals`, `debug_formatting_redacts_seat_pins_pin_entry_digits_and_notes`). ### 2.2 Three Issues in `preview_bundle_with_context` at `ed872e53` (Corrected per Review `B1–B2, C1, C3`)

### 2.3 Isolation Architecture: Crate-Level Boundary (`league-error`, `league-seat-types`, `league-seat-ui`, Binary Host `league-tui`), `ClubHost`, and `SeatObservation`

Every seat view (`league-seat-ui`, `ClubBot`, `league-relay`, and any future seat crate) receives strictly **`SeatObservation`** (`PublicSweepReceipt`, `PublicPreviewState`, `WhatIfPreview`, with `ClubHost::observe_seat` attaching strictly that seat's own `PrivateMandate`), constructed by value on the host side (`SeatObservation::own_mandate` is private and `with_own_mandate` has been removed):

- **Seat-boundary threat model (`2026-11-19`, per the PR #81 fix-4 review and the maintainer's ruling):**
  - **What the seat boundary protects against.** An honest builder leaking hidden state by mistake: seat code that names, holds or reads engine state, another seat's mandate, or a save file without meaning to. 1. **Q1 — AI-Seat Preview & Benchmark Arm (DECIDED `2026-11-17`):**
   - *Question raised by R&D's premise check (`:57-61`) and addendum:* Do AI seats receive an exact pre-commit preview tool, and should that tool be a declared Eval-condition arm rather than an unconditional change? (Moral/arithmetic-failure studies such as Morillo, Ferri, & Hernández-Orallo 2026, `25.2%` across `9,572` records, unchecked at source, via `2026-11-16-33-idle-seat-takeover-REPORT.md:384`, and `Belle et al., 2025`, unchecked at source, via `2026-11-16-31-hint-system-REPORT.md:16`, motivate separating strategy from arithmetic, while R&D flagged Eval baseline comparability.)
   - **Decision (`2026-11-17`, updated in Card 9 `2026-11-20`):** `league-relay` exposes `--preview-tool <off|on>`, **off by default**; when set to `on`, it runs as a separate named wording arm (`v2_preview_2026_11_17_r1` in Card 7; **`v2_preview_2026_11_20_r2`** in Card 9 while keeping `r1` registered and sealed) capped at **at most `3` preview calls per turn**. Built strictly on `preview_seat_bundle` over `PublicPreviewState` (`SweepMode::PreviewSteps0To7`, seat's own orders only, empty peers, neutral beacon; full `CounterpartyAssumptions` for AI seats remains only an unbuilt possible later arm).
2. **Q2 — `TableMode` Identifier for Offline Club Games (DECIDED `2026-11-17`, overrides initial draft):**
   - **Decision (`2026-11-17`):** Offline Club Mode uses a **distinct `TableMode::Club`** variant added in Card 2—never `TableMode::Eval`—so human Club games can never enter future automated Evaluation datasets (**stated requirement:** every future `Eval` dataset loader must require `log.mode == TableMode::Eval`, enforced in `league-core` by `ReplayRecord::from_eval_json_slice` and `replay_eval_season`). `TableMode::Club` runs with `ReplayBeaconMode::EvalVrf` proofs over OS-CSPRNG `SealedEvalSeed` and clear `SeedPool::Dev` (`0..=9_999`) layout seeds.
3. **Q3 — Club Mode vs. Testnet Game Mode Turn Clock (DECIDED `2026-11-17`):**
   - **Decision (`2026-11-17`):** Offline Club Mode defaults to **`210 s` (`3m30s`) per turn**, configurable across **`180–300 s`** (split across `H` human seats in Pass A / Pass B per §1.2), with early commit advance and an **Untimed** option; public-Testnet Game Mode keeps `10-minute` Live Club turns ([`docs/design-v2.md#L263`](design-v2.md#L263)).
4. **Q4 — Hot-Seat Order Flow & Pass Asymmetry (DECIDED `2026-11-17`):**
   - **Decision (`2026-11-17`):** Sequential **Two-Pass** hot-seat (`Pass A` open Phase 1 -> `begin_cancel_lock()` -> `Pass B` post-lock check and sealed commit, anchored to Wave 1 `DEFAULT_CANCEL_LOCK_BPS = 2_500`, `types.rs:149`, and closing-window evidence in `Dyhrberg et al., 2022`, unchecked at source, via `2026-11-16-32-end-of-season-valuation-REPORT.md:377`). While Step-0 uniform clearing removes arrival-order priority, sequential single-screen passes still leave later seats an intra-pass observation edge (equalized across turns by rotating the starting seat).
5. **Q5 — In-World Commodity Names Over Currency Codes (DECIDED `2026-11-17`):**
   - **Decision (`2026-11-17`):** `league-tui` renders role-qualified in-world guild names (`CommodityCatalog`) beside every 3-letter `[CODE]`, leaving on-ledger currency codes, `AssetId`, and `GENERATOR_VERSION` (`league-gen/v2.5.0`) unchanged.
6. **Q6 — Human Prose Hints vs. Predicate Gauges (DECIDED `2026-11-17`):**
   - **Decision (`2026-11-17`):** `v0.1.0` surfaces factual numeric `SeatStatePredicates` gauges alongside the interactive What-If Sandbox; templated three-level (`detailed` / `brief` / `off`) prose hints (discussed in `VanLehn, 2011`, `Renkl & Atkinson, 2003`, `Kalyuga, Ayres, Chandler, & Sweller, 2003`, unchecked at source, via `2026-11-16-31-hint-system-REPORT.md:3, 21-35`) are deferred until after `v0.1.0`.

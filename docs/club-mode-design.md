## 0. Scope, Premise Corrections, and Epistemic Rules

Under the `2026-11-17` scope ruling recorded in `#68` and [`docs/design-v2.md`](design-v2.md#L5) and the `2026-11-17` rulings on §6, `v0.1.0` is **Club Mode** (`TableMode::Club`)—an offline terminal season in `league-tui` over pure-Rust `league-core` with humans on one machine and simple rule-based bot seats, playing a single-human season (`H = 1`) in roughly `90 minutes` `[target]` at a `210 s` default turn clock (`180–300 s` configurable; multi-human tables add hand-off grace per §1.2)—and every `2026-11-17` design-review improvement tracked in `#68` lands before `1.0` in an order paced by the Publisher.

Three rules govern every figure and citation in this document:

1. **No simulated or newly computed figures:** Every quantitative value either cites its source (`path:line` at `ed872e53bc9381ac2e2f76167164173b113788f2`, arithmetic directly derived from those turn bounds, or a named report file) or is explicitly marked `[target]`.
2. **Unchecked external citations:** External academic or industry studies referenced through deep-research reports (seeds `25–33`) have **not** been audited at primary source here and are marked `(unchecked at source, via <report>)` whenever a design choice relies on them.
3. **Four premise corrections applied (`2026-11-17-rnd-premise-check-ml-design-advice.md` & addendum):**
   - *Preview built strictly on `league-core` simulation, never predicates:* `crates/league-core/src/predicates.rs:1-20` (`compute_seat_state_predicates` at `predicates.rs:625-642`) computes static start-of-turn diagnostics (*if nothing changes before Sweep*). At baseline `ed872e53` that path sat behind `TurnEngine::preview_bundle*`; after Wave 2 Card 2 / 2b, the interactive human What-If Sandbox builds strictly on [`preview_what_if`](../crates/league-core/src/observation.rs) (`SweepMode::PreviewSteps0To7` over `PublicPreviewState`) with caller-chosen counterparty assumptions (plus pre/post `SeatStatePredicates` gauges), and the optional AI-seat preview in arm `v2_preview_2026_11_17_r1` builds strictly on [`preview_seat_bundle`](../crates/league-core/src/observation.rs) (`SweepMode::PreviewSteps0To7`, seat's own orders only, empty peers, neutral beacon), never a second rules engine.
   - *Currency codes already draw from a 61-word English glossary:* [`ALLOWED_COMMODITY_GLOSSARY`](../crates/league-gen/src/schema.rs#L540-L602) already restricts generated 3-letter codes to `61` curated trade nouns (`ALM` = `"alum"`, `TMB` = `"timber"`) while rejecting uncurated strings such as `QZX` (`schema.rs:688-706`) and excluding `511` entries in [`DENIED_CURRENCY_CODES`](../crates/league-gen/src/schema.rs#L624-L677). Each table samples `2 * N` distinct codes (`8`, `10`, or `12` goods for `N in 4..=6` seats at `crates/league-gen/src/generator.rs:452, 563-570`) rather than a single paired raw/refined chain.
   - *Variable season horizon:* Seasons run `24..=32` turns (`CLOSING_ROLL_START_TURN = 24`, `HARD_TURN_CAP = 32`, `DEFAULT_CLOSING_HAZARD_BPS = [3_500; 8]`, expected length `25.80` turns and `3.19%` Turn-32 probability per [`types.rs`](../crates/league-core/src/types.rs#L62-L75) and [`docs/design-v2.md`](design-v2.md#L242-L245)). Expected duration therefore depends on the `210 s` default clock plus hot-seat hand-off grace across `H` human seats (§1.2).
   - *Benchmark comparability for AI preview:* Granting AI seats exact pre-commit simulation alters what automated Evaluation Mode measures (compressing arithmetic-error variance and shifting invalid-transaction / stability metrics in [`docs/design-v2.md`](design-v2.md#L310)), so AI preview access sits off the `v0.1.0` critical path (Card 7) behind `--preview-tool <off|on>` (`off` default) and named arm `v2_preview_2026_11_17_r1`.

### 1.4 Hot-Seat Privacy Rules, Sequential Passes, and Hand-Off Curtain

Per [`docs/design-v2.md`](design-v2.md#L104-L112) (`2026-11-16` Director ruling), visibility in `SeatObservation` is partitioned strictly:

- **Public on every seat's screen:** All seats' free balances, `cred_debt`, `credit_tier`, `PublicTableSpec` (including each seat's `Option<RegentDoctrine>`, which is **public table configuration on purpose**, `C4`), resting DEX offers (open and cartel domains), open checks/escrows/pledges, offices, charters, active realized shocks (`active_shocks`, carrying public `magnitude_bps` from `realize_turn`), already-announced `ShockForecastEvent`s (zero magnitude), historical `fill_log`, and `PublicSweepReceipt`s (`30` seat-safe fields only; **never** raw `SweepReceipt`, `newly_drawn_shocks`, pre-realize / drawn / forecast / hint shock magnitudes, or `post_state_digest`).
- **Private to Seat `i` only (carried outside public state):** Seat `i`'s `MandateReveal`, Seat `i`'s active `OracleAuditor` `AuditorShockHint` (`hint_turn <= turn < forecast_turn`, without `magnitude_bps`), Seat `i`'s whispers and personal notes, and Seat `i`'s uncommitted/unrevealed Phase-2 `SealedOrderBundle` draft and `PreimageSecret`s.

Because Sweep Step 0 (`step0_clear_phase1_offer_batch`, `engine.rs:1728-1941`) clears crossing Phase-1 `CredOffer`s by price priority plus beacon tie-breaks rather than submission timestamp, keyboard order grants zero arrival-order queue priority at clearing. Inside sequential hot-seat passes on one shared terminal, however, later seats within Pass A and Pass B observe earlier seats' newly posted public offers, checks, or post-lock imports before acting—an intra-pass information edge mitigated across the season by rotating starting order (`(turn - 1 + offset) % N`) rather than equaling simultaneous multi-terminal blindness (`B9` clarification). Each turn steps across:

- **Sub-Pass A (Open Market & Talk)** -> **`TurnEngine::begin_cancel_lock()` (`engine.rs:602-612`)** -> **Sub-Pass B (Post-Lock Check & Sealed Lock)** -> **Single Local Seal-and-Sweep** (`advance_to_commit`, `submit_order_commitment`, `advance_to_reveal`, `submit_order_reveal`, `sweep_turn_with_eval_vrf`).
- **Anti-holdout & Curtain:** In timed multi-human play, per-seat lock indicators stay hidden until pass close (informed by PlayDiplomacy holdout-sniping notes in `2026-11-16-33-idle-seat-takeover-REPORT.md:392`, unchecked at source). Pressing `Lock & Hand Off` or `Ctrl-L` drops Seat `i`'s `SeatObservation`, clears UI buffers, and renders an alternate-screen `CurtainScreen` showing only `TableId`, Turn `t`, pass label, and `"Hand keyboard to Seat k (<Guild Name>)"` until `[Space] + [Enter]` (plus PIN if configured).

### 1.5 Simple Bot Seats (`ClubBot` — Deterministic Rules-Only)

2. **Secure upkeep first (`UpkeepAndDebt`, `predicates.rs:43-58`):** Pull own resting sells of `upkeep_good` before cancel-lock if needed; bid up to `< town_import_premium_bps` (`2.25x`, `types.rs:137`); use `TownImport` only when shortfall remains and debt penalty exceeds import cost.

### 1.6 Save / Resume Threat Model and Secret Isolation

- **Threat model (`B4` settled choice):** Offline Club Mode save/resume guarantees **accidental-exposure screen privacy among honest hot-seat players sharing one terminal**; it does **not** defend against a local OS user with shell access to the same user account reading local process memory or host files.
- **No raw VRF seed on disk, and the 32 pre-derived VRF proofs live exclusively in `.seal` (`B4`):** Because `eval_vrf_alpha(table_id, Turn(t))` (`commit.rs:1333-1339`) depends solely on `(table_id, t)` for `t in 1..=HARD_TURN_CAP` (`32`), Lobby setup generates an ephemeral `SealedEvalSeed`, records `eval_vrf_pubkey`, pre-derives all `32` 80-byte `eval_vrf_proof`s (`2,560` bytes) upfront via `prove_turn`, and drops `SealedEvalSeed` in memory before Turn 1 starts. **Neither file ever writes a raw 32-byte `SealedEvalSeed` to disk.** However, because `verify_and_extract_eval_vrf_beacon` derives every turn's `turn_beacon` (`beta[..32]`) deterministically from `(eval_vrf_pubkey, proof_t)` and `draw_shocks_for_turn` / `close_roll_bps_from_beacon` depend solely on `turn_beacon`, **all `32` pre-derived `eval_vrf_proof`s are just as sensitive as `SealedEvalSeed` itself**—anyone holding them with `eval_vrf_pubkey` can compute every future beacon, every future shock draw, and every closing roll. Accordingly, the `32` pre-derived proofs go **strictly out of `.save.json`** and reside solely inside `.seal`.
- **What each file holds and who may open it in hot-seat play (`B4`, `C2`):**

## 6. Director Decisions on Design Questions (All Six Decided, `2026-11-17`)

1. **Q1 — AI-Seat Preview & Benchmark Arm (DECIDED `2026-11-17`):**
   - *Question raised by R&D's premise check (`:57-61`) and addendum:* Do AI seats receive an exact pre-commit preview tool, and should that tool be a declared Eval-condition arm rather than an unconditional change? (Moral/arithmetic-failure studies such as Morillo, Ferri, & Hernández-Orallo 2026, `25.2%` across `9,572` records, unchecked at source, via `2026-11-16-33-idle-seat-takeover-REPORT.md:384`, and `Belle et al., 2025`, unchecked at source, via `2026-11-16-31-hint-system-REPORT.md:16`, motivate separating strategy from arithmetic, while R&D flagged Eval baseline comparability.)
   - **Decision (`2026-11-17`):** `league-relay` exposes `--preview-tool <off|on>`, **off by default**; when set to `on`, it runs as a separate named wording arm **`v2_preview_2026_11_17_r1`** capped at **at most `3` preview calls per turn**. Built strictly on `preview_seat_bundle` over `PublicPreviewState` (`SweepMode::PreviewSteps0To7`, seat's own orders only, empty peers, neutral beacon; full `CounterpartyAssumptions` for AI seats remains only an unbuilt possible later arm).
2. **Q2 — `TableMode` Identifier for Offline Club Games (DECIDED `2026-11-17`, overrides initial draft):**
   - **Decision (`2026-11-17`):** Offline Club Mode uses a **distinct `TableMode::Club`** variant added in Card 2—never `TableMode::Eval`—so human Club games can never enter future automated Evaluation datasets (**stated requirement:** every future `Eval` dataset loader must require `log.mode == TableMode::Eval`, enforced in `league-core` by `ReplayRecord::from_eval_json_slice` and `replay_eval_season`). `TableMode::Club` runs with `ReplayBeaconMode::EvalVrf` proofs over OS-CSPRNG `SealedEvalSeed` and clear `SeedPool::Dev` (`0..=9_999`) layout seeds.
3. **Q3 — Club Mode vs. Testnet Game Mode Turn Clock (DECIDED `2026-11-17`):**
   - **Decision (`2026-11-17`):** Offline Club Mode defaults to **`210 s` (`3m30s`) per turn**, configurable across **`180–300 s`** (split across `H` human seats in Pass A / Pass B per §1.2), with early commit advance and an **Untimed** option; public-Testnet Game Mode keeps `10-minute` Live Club turns ([`docs/design-v2.md#L263`](design-v2.md#L263)).
4. **Q4 — Hot-Seat Order Flow & Pass Asymmetry (DECIDED `2026-11-17`):**
   - **Decision (`2026-11-17`):** Sequential **Two-Pass** hot-seat (`Pass A` open Phase 1 -> `begin_cancel_lock()` -> `Pass B` post-lock check and sealed commit, anchored to Wave 1 `DEFAULT_CANCEL_LOCK_BPS = 2_500`, `types.rs:149`, and closing-window evidence in `Dyhrberg et al., 2022`, unchecked at source, via `2026-11-16-32-end-of-season-valuation-REPORT.md:377`). While Step-0 uniform clearing removes arrival-order priority, sequential single-screen passes still leave later seats an intra-pass observation edge (equalized across turns by rotating the starting seat).
5. **Q5 — In-World Commodity Names Over Currency Codes (DECIDED `2026-11-17`):**
   - **Decision (`2026-11-17`):** `league-tui` renders role-qualified in-world guild names (`CommodityCatalog`) beside every 3-letter `[CODE]`, leaving on-ledger currency codes, `AssetId`, and `GENERATOR_VERSION` (`league-gen/v2.5.0`) unchanged.
6. **Q6 — Human Prose Hints vs. Predicate Gauges (DECIDED `2026-11-17`):**
   - **Decision (`2026-11-17`):** `v0.1.0` surfaces factual numeric `SeatStatePredicates` gauges alongside the interactive What-If Sandbox; templated three-level (`detailed` / `brief` / `off`) prose hints (discussed in `VanLehn, 2011`, `Renkl & Atkinson, 2003`, `Kalyuga, Ayres, Chandler, & Sweller, 2003`, unchecked at source, via `2026-11-16-31-hint-system-REPORT.md:3, 21-35`) are deferred until after `v0.1.0`.

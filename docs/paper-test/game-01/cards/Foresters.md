# Your card: the Foresters (game 1)

- **Home:** Hath
- **Converter:** 1 timber → 1 charcoal; you cut 2 timber free on any day your caravan is in Hath; up to 2 batches a day, at home
- **Upgrade:** better axes: spend 1 tools once, then cut 3 timber a day
- **Play only from this conversation.** Read no files, and use nothing but this brief, this card and the referee's messages.
- **Purse:** 60 coin. **Goods:** none. **Standing:** 0. Your caravan starts in Hath.

## Your mandates (secret)

- Deliver charcoal or timber to works in 3 different towns. **4 points.**
- Be the largest contributor to the Varn sea wall. **6 points.**

## This game

- **Seated:** Saltworks, Weavers, Smiths, Foresters.
- **Sitting out:** Dyers, Tanners.

| Town | Sells |

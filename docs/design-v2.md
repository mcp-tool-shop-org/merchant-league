# Merchant League v2 Design: The Chiral Concordat

**Status:** Draft specification for Part 1 of the Merchant League v2 build-out, translated directly from the seed-26 research specification (*Designing a Dual-Purpose On-Ledger Strategy Game and LLM Multi-Agent Benchmark*) and checked against live XRPL Mainnet and Testnet `feature` RPC state on **2026-11-15**. Supersedes `docs/design.md` (`main` and PR #6 `design/redesign-r1`), pending the Director's rulings on the named conflicts in [Section 9](#9-conflicts-with-docsdesignmd-and-decisions-for-the-director).

**v0.1.0 Scope Ruling (Director and Publisher, `2026-11-17`):** `v0.1.0` is **Club Mode** (an offline terminal season over `league-core`, specified in [`docs/club-mode-design.md`](club-mode-design.md) and [Section 7.5](#75-offline-club-mode-v010-local-terminal-over-league-core-2026-11-17)); every Carbon design item tracked in #68 goes in before 1.0, paced by the Publisher.

Merchant League v2 is one shared politico-economic game architecture serving two execution modes over identical C++ `xrpld` protocol rules without smart contracts:
1. **Game Mode (Human and Mixed Human–AI Play):** runs on the public XRPL Testnet (`s.altnet.rippletest.net`, `network_id = 1`), backed by an off-ledger event-sourced Archivist mirror and an automated 3-wave Phoenix state rehydrator that survives unconsented Testnet resets.
2. **Headless Training and Evaluation Mode (AI League and Benchmark):** runs on local containerized `xrpld` nodes in Stand-Alone Mode (`xrpld -a --start`), closing ledgers in milliseconds via the admin `ledger_accept` RPC so multi-agent reinforcement learning, heuristic baselines, and mirrored benchmark suites run free of public rate limits and network wipes.

## Hard Invariants and Epistemic Rules

1. **Testnet-only for public network play; no real money:** Live player-facing code connects strictly to XRPL Testnet endpoints and enforces `require_testnet` (`network_id == Some(1)`) in `league-ledger`. There is no real money, no real-currency pricing, and no off-platform transfer of game standing; all game copy uses game terms (`CRED`, marks, raw goods, refined goods, syndicate projects, table equity) and must pass `scripts/check-testnet-only.sh` and `scripts/check-copy.sh` with zero allow markers. (Note: how the Part 4 Stand-Alone `xrpld` harness enforces loopback + non-Mainnet `!= Some(0)` alongside `league-ledger::Testnet` is flagged as Conflict 11 for the Director.)
2. **Mainnet-enabled XRPL features only:** Every transaction type, flag, and amendment relied upon by v2 must be verified `enabled: true` on XRPL Mainnet via `feature` RPC before use, because Testnet resets align Testnet amendments with Mainnet. Testnet-only or unactivated proposals (`ConfidentialTransfer` XLS-96, `MPTokensV2`, unimplemented MPT DEX trading) are forbidden.
3. **Report figures are hypotheses, not facts:** All quantitative projections in the seed-26 report—random bilateral lock-in probabilities (`79.7%` and `64.2%`), Herfindahl-Hirschman Index comparisons (`0.655` / `1.53` effective dyads vs. `0.130` / `7.72` effective dyads), benchmark sample reductions (`372` vs. `64` vs. `~22` games), `~36.8 XRP` 6-seat table reserves, `~12–15 s` 3-wave Phoenix rehydration across `~95` inner transactions, context-compaction token reductions (`23.90M` to `3.29M` input tokens / `86.2%`), Rath's `73`-interaction drift onset (derived from simulated enterprise workflows, not strategy games), and Mode A / Mode B Playtest 1 target bands—are **engineering priors and hypotheses**. Parts 2–4 and Playtest 1 must measure them empirically before treating any number as settled.
4. **Unverified external references and legal claims:** Several 2026 citations in the seed-26 report (including CT-Bench, QUACK, MafiaScope, Zhang 2026, Kaggle Game Arena 2026, Wang et al. NSR 2026, and Jiang et al. 2025) have not yet been audited at source by R&D, and no peer-reviewed study has measured human–LLM coalition dynamics across multi-week asynchronous seasons. The EU AI Act Article 50(1) disclosure note is a legal claim requiring an external check before any public EU launch.
5. **Deterministic replayability:** Every turn transition, Steward bond ruling, check bounce, clawback, tariff shift, shock draw, and final equity score must replay identically from a table's initial seed plus its ordered log of committed/revealed actions and beacon/oracle outputs.

### Resolving the Six Structural Conflicts Between Human Fun and AI Benchmarking

| # | Conflict | Human Strategy Requirement | AI Training & Eval Requirement | v2 Chiral Concordat Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kingmaking vs. Psychometric Signal | Betrayal invites credible late-game retaliation and political drama | Resist arbitrary dead-player griefing that injects noise into ratings | **Cardinal Net Equity + Private Mandates:** Continuous equity scoring replaces binary solo win/loss so every seat retains positive marginal incentive through the final turn |
| 2 | Multi-Week Pacing vs. Drift & Compute | 12–16 day saga playable in short daily check-ins | High episode throughput while controlling long-horizon context drift and token cost | **Clock-Agnostic 24–32 Turn Seasons:** Two 12-hour turns/day (or 10-minute club turns) for humans; millisecond `ledger_accept` in Stand-Alone Mode for AI |
| 3 | Expressive Chat vs. Objective Auditing | Open rhetoric, bluffing, and coalition persuasion | Ground-truth verification free of noisy LLM judges, prompt injection, and steganography | **Typed Intent Header + Free-Text Body:** Structured JSON proposal headers referencing exact XRPL object IDs/vote targets paired with concise off-ledger chat hashed into `Memos` |
| 4 | Faction Asymmetry vs. Seat Variance | Distinct asymmetric economic roles | Low seat-bias variance and small sample size for model comparisons | **Rotational Chiral Base + Mirrored Duplicate Seeds:** Rotationally symmetric converter ring with modular `Credentials`, evaluated across seat-swapped duplicate runs |
| 5 | Non-Transitive Politics vs. Scalar Ranks | Shifting table metas and counter-coalitions | Clone-invariant ratings across cyclic matchups where Elo breaks down | **Dual Rating Layer:** Bayesian TrueSkill tiers displayed to human players; Coarse Correlated Equilibrium (CCE) Deviation Ratings on held-out populations for AI |
| 6 | Dramatic Shocks vs. Sample Efficiency | Supply disruptions that fracture static mega-alliances | Low luck variance so skill separates cleanly in ~20–30 matches | **Telegraphed Horizon Oracles + Control Variates:** 2-turn-ahead `OracleSet` shock forecasts on identical duplicate seeds scored with AIVAT-style control variates |

## 1. The Chiral Converter Ring and Economy (`N = 4, 5, 6` Seats)

### 1.1 Elimination of Spatial Travel and Random Endowments

Early prototype playtests stalled because random resource endowments frequently allowed two seats to form a closed bilateral swap loop while multi-turn spatial travel locked up working capital across empty turns. Merchant League v2 removes spatial caravan movement across towns entirely and replaces random endowments with a **directed chiral converter ring** over $N \in \{4, 5, 6\}$ seats indexed modulo $N$ ($i \in \{0, \dots, N-1\}$).

### 1.2 Token Representation (Trust Line IOUs Only)

- **No Multi-Purpose Tokens (MPTs) for commodities:** As of November 2026, `MPTokensV1` does not implement DEX `OfferCreate` order-book trading or AMMs. All tradable game commodities and the table numeraire coin `CRED` are issued as **XRPL Trust Line Tokens (IOUs)** by a single per-table **Authority (Steward)** account using table-scoped 3-character currency codes (`CRED`, Raw $R_0 \dots R_{N-1}$, Refined $M_0 \dots M_{N-1}$). Who may see these balances is set once in [What One Seat May Know About Another](#what-one-seat-may-know-about-another-director-ruling-2026-11-16).
- **Mandatory genesis account-flag order (on empty Authority directory):**
  1. `asfRequireAuth` — restricts token custody exclusively to seated accounts authorized for that table, preventing cross-table token leakage.
  2. `asfAllowTrustLineClawback` — **must be enabled before any trust line or owner-directory object exists** (fails permanently otherwise, and must never be combined with `asfNoFreeze`, which returns `tecNO_PERMISSION`). Enables automated spoilage and upkeep sinks.
  3. `asfAllowTrustLineLocking` — enables `TokenEscrow` (`XLS-85`) for IOU bonds and conditional vaults.
  4. `asfDefaultRipple` on Authority, paired with `NoRipple` (`tfSetNoRipple`) on every seat's trust lines so seats cannot involuntarily bridge third-party balances.
- **Auto-bridging faucet-drain prevention:** Because XRPL DEX `OfferCreate` auto-bridges `Token A -> XRP -> Token B` whenever composite XRP pairs are cheaper—and Testnet faucet XRP is free—trading on an open XRP pair would let any player drain scarce commodities with free XRP. Every seat and Authority sets `asfDisallowXRP` after initial reserve funding, all game markets price strictly in `CRED`, and all cartel books are isolated inside `PermissionedDomains` (`DomainID` auto-bridges strictly within the same domain).

### 1.3 Three-Tier Chiral Production and Upkeep Cycle

Every turn, each Seat $i \in \{0, \dots, N-1\}$ operates three economic tiers on the directed ring (indices modulo $N$):

1. **Tier 1 — Raw Extraction:** Seat $i$ receives turn yield $y_i > 0$ of its exclusive raw commodity $R_i$ (minted by Authority at Sweep).
2. **Tier 2 — Per-Seat Converter Recipes and Tiered Guild Workshops (2026-11-15 Director rulings):** Seat $i$ operates guild workshops converting strictly foreign raw inputs into its Refined commodity $M_i$, settled atomically on-ledger via `BatchV1_1` (`tfAllOrNothing`), delivering inputs to Authority (burn/sink) and minting $c_i M_i$. Rather than sampling one shared stoichiometry range for every seat, the generator draws overlapping stratified per-seat input splits, batch scales, and capacity caps so seats' workshop recipes differ structurally across the ring. Choosing Route `(b)` (capacity-binding guild workshops rather than a second raw good per seat) retains one exclusive raw commodity per seat (`2N` commodity currencies total on-ledger, keeping XRPL trust-line and tariff schedules bounded) while activating positive conversion scarcity rents (`converter_cap_duals > 0`). Under the `2026-11-15` round-2 Director decision, Route `(b)`'s extra option is explicitly accepted as side-income scarcity rent on capped workshops (run alongside foreign raw sales) rather than requiring a seat's own workshop to be its top-ranked trade, and the $N = 5$ four-seat exclusion bound is kept at `2/4` (`50%`) of members:
   - **$N = 4$:** one capacity-binding primary workshop $(R_{(i+1)\bmod N}, R_{(i+2)\bmod N})$ (`is_secondary = false`) plus one higher-cost clearing secondary cross-converter $(R_{(i+2)\bmod N}, R_{(i+3)\bmod N})$ (`is_secondary = true`), giving each seat `5` positive-value shadow-LP options (`Own` primary capacity rent plus `4` foreign raw-supply lanes).
   - **$N = 5$:** one capacity-binding primary workshop $(R_{(i+1)\bmod 5}, R_{(i+2)\bmod 5})$ (`is_secondary = false`), one capacity-binding 3-input intermediate workshop $(R_{(i+1)\bmod 5}, R_{(i+2)\bmod 5}, R_{(i+3)\bmod 5})$ (`is_secondary = true`), and one clearing secondary cross-converter $(R_{(i+2)\bmod 5}, R_{(i+4)\bmod 5})$ (`is_secondary = true`), giving each seat `9` positive-value shadow-LP options across `6` distinct foreign raw-supply labels and `2` own-workshop capacity rents. Requiring $R_{(i+2)\bmod 5}$ in every $N = 5$ workshop blocks converter owner $(k + 2)\bmod 5$ whenever seat $(k + 4)\bmod 5$ is excluded, keeping every 4-seat bloc at `<= 2` of `4` members fed.
   - **$N = 6$:** two capacity-binding guild workshops $(R_{(i+1)\bmod 6}, R_{(i+2)\bmod 6})$ (`is_secondary = false`) and $(R_{(i+1)\bmod 6}, R_{(i+3)\bmod 6})$ (`is_secondary = true`), paired with one clearing secondary cross-converter $(R_{(i+2)\bmod 6}, R_{(i+4)\bmod 6})$ (`is_secondary = true`), giving each seat `8` positive-value shadow-LP options (`Own` rents on both binding workshops plus `6` distinct foreign raw-supply lanes), with even ring step differences (`S1(1, 3)` and `S2(2, 4)`) keeping clearing cycle matrices well-conditioned symmetrically around balanced input splits.
   In economic terms, each seat's capacity-binding primary and intermediate workshops represent scarce specialized guild facilities earning positive conversion margin at competitive shadow duals, while the clearing cross-converter absorbs residual raw extraction at positive value-added so perishable raw goods are not stranded. Every workshop consumes strictly foreign raw inputs and never consumes opposite neighbor pairs $\{(i-1)\bmod N, (i+1)\bmod N\}$.
3. **Tier 3 — Syndicate Projects / Works (Irreversible Equity Sinks & `CRED` Recycling Stipend, Director Decision `C3`, `2026-11-15`):** Multi-commodity Syndicate Projects consume specified bundles of Refined goods $(M_0, \dots, M_{N-1})$ transferred to Authority (destroyed on return to issuer) and award irreversible **Project Equity** points. Under Director Decision `C3` (`2026-11-15`), Works deliveries also recycle `CRED` into circulation by minting a provisional fixed coin stipend (`DEFAULT_WORKS_STIPEND_PER_UNIT_CRED = 350` milli-`CRED` per `1_000` milli-units of refined goods delivered, configurable on `TableSpec.works_stipend_per_unit_cred`) directly to the delivering seat, offsetting ongoing `CRED` burns without ever converting coin holdings into Victory Points.
4. **Town Import Supply Fallback (Director Decision `C1b`, `2026-11-15`):** Because a withholding supplier can temporarily starve downstream converters or Works deliveries even on a 4-seat ring, any seat may use Phase-1 `TownImport` to buy commodity units directly from Authority at a provisional premium multiplier over fundamental parity (`DEFAULT_TOWN_IMPORT_PREMIUM_BPS = 22_500`, i.e., `2.25x` parity via `ceil(amount * parity * premium_bps / (UNIT_SCALE * BPS_SCALE))`, configurable via `TableSpec.town_import_premium_bps` as a fallback rather than a primary trade channel).
5. **Chiral Upkeep and Gate-Tested Coalition Bounds (2026-11-15 Director decision):** At the end of every turn, Seat $i$ owes mandatory upkeep $u_i > 0$ in the refined good produced by its opposite neighbor $M_{(i-1) \bmod N}$:
   - Seat $i$ extracts $R_i$, cannot convert its own raw good, and owes upkeep in $M_{(i-1) \bmod N}$ (which Seat $i-1$ produces strictly from foreign raw goods without enclosing any self-sufficient pair).
   - Every coalition bound stated here is enforced by gate tests over generated tables across $N \in \{4, 5, 6\}$ (`>= 600` seeds per $N$): **1-seat autarky feeds `0` of `1` member** (`0%`, LP coverage `<= 1e-7`); **any 2-seat dyad feeds `0` of `2` members** (`0%`, LP coverage `<= 1e-7`); **any 3-seat bloc across $N \in \{4, 5, 6\}$ feeds at most `1` of `3` members unweighted** (LP-weighted upkeep coverage bounded by that single member's share of the 3-bloc's upkeep plus `1e-6` LP tolerance, and `<= 1/3 + 0.006` under `±0.875%` per-seat upkeep dispersion); and **any 4-seat bloc across $N \in \{5, 6\}$ feeds at most `2` of `4` members unweighted** (`50%` of members, with LP-weighted upkeep coverage bounded by those two members' share of the 4-bloc's upkeep plus `1e-6` LP tolerance, and `<= 0.5 + 0.005` under `±0.875%` per-seat upkeep dispersion).

*Hypotheses to verify in Part 2:* The seed-26 report models that random 2-of-4 resource endowments in a 5-seat table produce a `79.7%` probability of a closed 100% self-sufficient 2-seat dyad (`64.2%` probability of $\ge 75\%$ mutual coverage with 6 resources), and projects a trade-dispersion shift from bilateral lock-in (`HHI = 0.655`, `1.53` effective pairs of 10) to chiral ring play (`HHI = 0.130`, `7.72` effective pairs). Part 2's generator crate must measure these distributions directly rather than assuming them.

### 1.4 Perishable Decay and Tiered Auto-Debt Cushion

- **Perishable spoilage (`Clawback`):** Unconverted Raw goods $R_k$ (and designated perishable outputs) decay at the end of every turn at the table's spoilage rate (baseline `20%`/turn, rounded deterministically). Because XRPL `Clawback` cannot seize tokens locked inside `TokenEscrow` (`docs/receipts/design-check-requireauth-2026-11-14/`), **perishable commodities are never eligible to be locked in multi-turn `TokenEscrow` collateral vaults**. Collateralized vaults and bonds lock only non-perishable `CRED`. Authority always queries the current trust-line balance before submitting `Clawback` and logs the exact clawed-back amount (balances are public; see [What One Seat May Know About Another](#what-one-seat-may-know-about-another-director-ruling-2026-11-16)).
- **Auto-debt cushion (preventing death spirals):** If Seat $i$ lacks sufficient $M_{(i-1) \bmod N}$ at Sweep upkeep collection, Authority claws back any available $M_{(i-1) \bmod N}$ and converts the unpaid shortfall at fundamental parity into interest-bearing `CRED` debt owed to the clearinghouse. Debt accrues per-turn interest set by the seat's on-ledger credit rating credential:
  - `CREDIT_AAA`: `4%`/turn interest (report cites `2%–6%` range; `4%` baseline).
  - `CREDIT_BBB` / `CREDIT_B`: intermediate stepped tiers (e.g., `10%` and `18%`/turn).
  - `CREDIT_D` (defaulted): `30%`/turn interest plus immediate exclusion from prime cartel `PermissionedDomains`.
  Auto-debt prevents early accidental elimination while `CREDIT_D` penalties block deliberate "debt diving" exploits.

### 1.5 Procedural Table Generator and Feasibility Filter (Part 2 Specification)

Static table layouts invite opening-book memorization. Part 2 builds a pure-Rust, ledger-free seeded table generator paired (in Training/Eval Mode) with **Prioritized Level Replay (PLR)** sampling across five table parameters:
1. Seat count $N \in \{4, 5, 6\}$.
2. Per-seat stratified converter recipes $(a_{i,k}, b_{i,k} \to c_{i,k})$ and tiered workshop run capacities (capacity-binding primary/intermediate guild workshops plus a clearing secondary cross-converter).
3. Baseline commodity export `TransferRate` tariffs (`0%–20%` initial baseline; dynamic minister bounds `0%–35%`).
4. Perishable `Clawback` decay rates.
5. `OracleSet` shock schedules and Syndicate Project requirements.

Before any generated table seed is accepted for play or training, a deterministic **Linear-Programming (LP) Feasibility Filter** must prove all three invariants:
1. **Zero 2-seat self-sufficiency:** For every unordered pair $\{i, j\}$, internal extraction plus pair-internal conversion cannot satisfy both seats' turn upkeep (`0%` pair self-sufficiency).
2. **Strict aggregate converter surplus:** Table-wide feasible production strictly exceeds aggregate turn upkeep by a positive project-building margin (and positive per-converter clearing margin) so cooperative trade is strictly positive-sum.
3. **Seat equity balance:** Under optimal competitive/cooperative shadow-price allocation, every seat $i \in \{0, \dots, N-1\}$ starts with expected potential within `±3%` (`ev_tolerance = 0.03`) of table mean baseline. In addition, under the `2026-11-16` Director ruling, any candidate table where a seat's exact `2^N` sub-coalition Shapley value deviates by more than `7%` (`SHAPLEY_GUARD_MAX_REL_DEV = 0.07`) from the seats' Shapley mean is refused, catching a seat holding a sub-coalition bottleneck that the grand-coalition shadow prices miss. This fairness check stays strictly a fair-start filter with stationary attempt-0 sampling (no annealing and no rejection filtering for low variety).

Under the `2026-11-15` G1 structural variety ruling, generator output is additionally verified against three quantitative variety floors at each $N \in \{4, 5, 6\}$ (`>= 600` seeds per $N$):
1. **Within-table dispersion:** within-table coefficient of variation satisfies `yield intra_cv >= 0.0038` and `tariff intra_cv >= 0.20` with across-$N$ spread `(max - min) / min <= 10%`.
2. **Distinct best-trade orderings (`D1`):** evaluating each seat's `>= 3` positive options at the shadow LP dual solution (`Own` converter capacity rent `x_conv * y_cap` and `Supply` raw delivery `x_conv * qty * (1 + raw_tariff) * y_raw`, filtering values at or below `1e-9 * seat_max`), labeling options ring-relatively as `(owner_offset, is_secondary, Own | Supply)`, and sorting by descending value to take the top-3 ordering, at least `90%` of generated tables at every $N \in \{4, 5, 6\}$ must give all $N$ seats pairwise-distinct top-3 orderings.
3. **Between-table spread:** inter-table first-seat yield and tariff CVs are preserved at or above the `main` baseline.

In Headless Training Mode, PLR weights replay priority toward valid seeds exhibiting high policy value-loss / regret variance while preserving non-zero coverage of fresh seeds.

## 2. Simultaneous Turn Engine: Talk & Market, Commit, Sweep

Sequential turns impose an $(N-1)/N$ waiting penalty and let backward induction trivialize bilateral bargains. Merchant League v2 uses **simultaneous discrete turns** modeled on Diplomacy and Subterfuge, executed identically in pure state (Part 3) and on-ledger (later card):

### What One Seat May Know About Another (Director ruling, `2026-11-16`)

This is the one place the game states what is visible between seats; other sections refer here rather than restating it.

- **Public to every seat:** every seat's free balances (raw goods, refined goods, `CRED`, auto-debt) and its credit tier, and every resting offer, including the resting offers in a cartel domain's book, which seats outside the domain can also see.
- **Private until the rules reveal it:** a seat's mandate card (and its commitment, until the close reveals the card), negotiation text, committed-but-unrevealed orders, randomness not yet published (a Horizon Shock hint reaches only the Oracle Auditor until the public forecast, §5.2 and §6.1) and private notes.
- **Why:** balances and resting offers sit on a public ledger, and any seat's software can read them there. A rule that called them private could not be enforced, so the rule makes them public. What stays private is what never reaches the ledger in the clear: off-ledger text, salted commitments and unpublished draws.
- **What this does not settle:** which of this public information a seat is actually shown, and in what form, is a separate decision for each client and each model wording; the ruling sets only what a seat may know.

### Phase 1: Diplomatic and Market Window ("Talk and Market")

- **Dual-format negotiation messages (off-ledger Relay):** Every negotiation message pairs:
  1. A **Typed JSON Intent Header** naming exact counterparties, commodity quantities, `CRED` amounts, deadlines, referenced XRPL object IDs (`Offer`, `Escrow`, `Check` sequence/ID), or council vote targets.
  2. A concise **Free-Text Body** (`<120` words per message in Game Mode) carrying rhetoric, explanations, and persuasion.
  - Raw negotiation text is **never written onto the public XRPL ledger** (GDPR and transcript-privacy rule; it stays private under [What One Seat May Know About Another](#what-one-seat-may-know-about-another-director-ruling-2026-11-16)). Only salted SHA-256 hashes of committed deal digests may appear in XRPL `Memos` ($\le 1\text{ KB}$ total per transaction).
- **Live ledger market actions & simultaneous batch clearing (Director Decision `C4`, `2026-11-15`; C4 amended `2026-11-17`):** Throughout the Phase-1 window, seats post or cancel `OfferCreate`/`OfferCancel` orders in `CRED` (within cartel `DomainID` or open/hybrid `tfHybrid` books), use `TownImport` fallback supplies from Authority (`C1b`), create deferred `CheckCreate` credit notes, or post collateralized `TokenEscrow` vaults. Posting an offer validates domain access and locks the buyer's worst-case `CRED` outlay or seller's offered commodity units upfront without crossing immediately in arrival order (refunded when cancelled). Instead, Sweep Step 0 executes **simultaneous batch clearing** (`C4`): across each commodity book, eligible non-frozen offers are ordered strictly by price priority (`Buy` descending limit, `Sell` ascending limit) with tied prices across distinct seats ordered deterministically from the turn's public beacon rather than submission arrival time; compatible pairs cross greedily at price priority and settle within each `(good, domain)` batch at the uniform Walrasian midpoint of the marginal crossed limits (`floor((max_crossed_sell + min_crossed_buy) / 2)` clamped inside each fill's limits), refunding any excess locked `CRED` to the buyer and crediting net commodity deliveries after export `TransferRate` deduction.
  - **Cancel lock (`C4` amended, Director `2026-11-17`):** Because bids already lock real `CRED` (refunded on cancel), `C4` already removed arrival-order advantage, and the closing turn is a hidden stochastic roll (§6.2), Phase 1 adds a logged cancel lock: `TableSpec.cancel_lock_bps` (`DEFAULT_CANCEL_LOCK_BPS = 2_500`, the last `25%` of Phase 1; `0` disables the lock; provisional, pending playtest calibration) governs when the orchestrator engages `TurnEngine::begin_cancel_lock()` (`LoggedTurn.cancel_lock_at: Option<u32>` in deterministic replay logs; logs omitting it replay as before). Once engaged until `advance_to_commit`, `CancelOffer` fails with `EngineError::CancelLocked` (`EngineError::code() == "CANCEL_LOCKED"`, recorded per turn in `ReplayOutcome.rejected_market_actions` when non-empty), while `SeatStatePredicates.cancel_locked` (`STATE_PREDICATES_VERSION = 3`) exposes the flag publicly. Engine-initiated cancellations (`cancel_domain_offers_for_seat`, expiry, spoilage) are exempt. Because the lock leaves `PostOffer` open, it does not block late bids directly; unhittable closing-turn high bids (`created_turn >= turn`) and `< 1.000`-unit (`< UNIT_SCALE`) resting bids are instead excluded by §4.1's close-marking rule (`#54`). **Out of scope for `league-core`:** on-ledger enforcement in Game mode must later map the cancel lock onto a validated ledger close time.
- **Parallel non-stalling submission (`TicketBatch`):** Seats pre-allocate XRPL `Tickets` (`TicketCreate`, `Sequence: 0`) so multiple independent offers, checks, and commits per turn never suffer cascading `Sequence`-number failures if one transaction fails.

### Phase 2: Simultaneous Cryptographic Commit Cutoff ("Commit")

- **Sealed order bundle:** Before the turn deadline, each seat submits one cryptographic commitment for its simultaneous discretionary actions:
  - Converter runs (`Tier 2`) and Syndicate Project deliveries (`Tier 3`).
  - Sealed-bid Charter auction bids (Turns 6, 12, 18, 24, 30).
  - Hidden-value trade challenges and escrow preimage handover commits.
  - Council election and tariff votes (Turns 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32).
- **On-ledger commit-reveal primitive:** Lock 1 drop (or deal collateral) in `EscrowCreate` with `Condition = PREIMAGE-SHA-256` and a salted SHA-256 digest in `Memos` ($\le 1\text{ KB}$), then reveal by submitting `EscrowFinish` with `Fulfillment` (paying the elevated XRPL fulfillment fee floor, $\ge 330$ drops) at the reveal gate. Pure-state simulation (Part 3) models the identical `(commitment_hash, reveal_payload, salt)` envelope deterministically with no ledger I/O.
- **Standing Regent Delegation (`PermissionDelegationV1_1`, including Director Decision `C1a`, `2026-11-15` & `2026-11-16`):** To prevent absent or persistently idle humans ("Civil Disorder") from starving downstream ring neighbors of $R_i$ or $M_i$, each human seat may pre-authorize a scoped **Standing Regent Agent** via XRPL `DelegateSet` (`PermissionDelegationV1_1`) without exposing its master seed. The Regent is strictly scoped by a 3-slider player Doctrine (risk tolerance, minimum conversion reserve, maximum credit spread) to run upkeep converters, cash maturing Checks/Escrows, and maintain standing quotes. After **3 consecutive missed turn cutoffs** — or **`IDLE_MISS_TAKEOVER_TURNS = 3` consecutive post-Turn-1 turns (`t >= 2`; Turn 1 is excluded because seats start with `CRED` only before Step 4 mints initial raw yields) in which the seat executed zero converter batches AND made no delivery, no DEX batch fill, and no Works delivery** — the seat counts as missed, invokes its Standing Regent fallback (excluding committed non-revealers per `T4`), and converts to a disclosed `SEAT_AI_SYNDICATE` seat.
- **Non-reveal penalty (EIP-4399 + `T4` ruling, `2026-11-15`):** Any seat that fails to submit a valid reveal matching its Phase 2 commitment forfeits its sealed discretionary actions for that turn and **never triggers a table re-roll**. Under Director ruling `T4` (`2026-11-15`), Standing Regent fallback applies **only** to seats that did not submit an unrevealed Phase 2 commitment on that turn; committed non-revealers receive neither discretionary actions nor Regent execution even on their third zero-production turn.

### Phase 3: Deterministic Resolution Sweep ("Sweep")

At the turn cutoff, the Turn Engine (pure-state in Part 3; Authority/Steward + ledger transactions on-chain) executes **nine deterministic steps (Step 0 plus Steps 1–8) in fixed order**:

0. **Clear Phase-1 Offer Batch (`C4`, amended `2026-11-17`):** Simultaneously clear crossable resting DEX bids and asks by price priority with beacon-ordered tie-breaks at the uniform batch midpoint price (after any `CancelOffer` cutoff under the Phase-1 cancel lock), deducting export tariffs and unlocking surplus buyer `CRED`.
1. **Validate Reveals:** Verify each Phase 2 reveal against its Phase 1/2 SHA-256 commitment; mark unrevealed or malformed seats as non-reveals and apply default fallback behavior for non-committers.

## 3. Dual-Track Contracts and Two-Part Reputation

Requiring every multi-turn contract to lock 100% collateral freezes illiquid seats, while making every promise unenforceable collapses multi-turn trade into spot barter. Merchant League v2 separates contracts into two explicit tracks:

| Track | Mechanism | On-Ledger Primitive | Collateral & Default Semantics | Reputation Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Track 1: Ledger-Enforced** | Atomic multi-leg swaps & converter runs | `BatchV1_1` (`tfAllOrNothing`, up to 8 inner txs) | **Zero default risk:** all legs settle or none do | Honors commitment if executed |
| **Track 1: Ledger-Enforced** | Buyer delivery-versus-payment (`BuyerDvpPayment`), co-signed dual escrow (`DualEscrowVault`), preimage vaults & performance bonds | `TokenEscrow` (`PREIMAGE-SHA-256`, `FinishAfter`, `CancelAfter`) + Phase-2 `ContractDeliveryOrder` | **Capped at <= 100% combined `CRED` lock (Director Decision `C2`, `2026-11-15`):** replaces 200% mutual lockups. In `BuyerDvpPayment`, only the buyer locks `100%` of the purchase `CRED` (zero seller lock); once verified net deliveries reach `required_units` on or before `deadline_turn`, locked `CRED` releases straight to the seller, whereas missed seller delivery by the deadline cancels the escrow, refunds all locked `CRED` to the buyer, and logs a bond breach against the seller. In co-signed `DualEscrowVault`, buyer and seller lock positive bonds summing to `<= 100%` of `payment_cred`; fulfilling delivery settles `payment_cred` (using `buyer_bond_cred` plus buyer top-up) plus the seller's unlocked `seller_bond_cred`, while seller default transfers both locked bonds to the buyer and buyer default on the remaining top-up leaves the seller's goods untouched while transferring both locked bonds to the seller (recording a bond breach against whichever side defaulted) | Fulfilled contract settles cleanly; default by either side forfeits that party's locked bond and increments `bond_breaches` + `honor_penalties` |
| **Track 2: Breakable Credit** | Deferred working-capital loans & raw-input credit | `CheckCreate` (`SendMax`, `Expiration`) settled via `CheckCash` | **Zero upfront lockup; defaultable:** drawer can spend liquidity elsewhere before `CheckCash`, causing the Check to bounce | **Hard Credit Solvency:** bounce triggers automatic `CREDIT_AAA -> ... -> CREDIT_D` downgrade |
| **Track 2: Breakable Promises** | Council vote pledges, cartel admission/embargo pacts, preimage handovers | Signed Typed JSON Intent Header (hashed in `Memo`) | **Unenforced on-ledger:** counterparty can break vote or withhold off-ledger preimage | **Soft Diplomatic Honor:** deterministic promise-vs-ledger audit records betrayal $\Delta\text{EV}$ |

Every bilateral deal therefore becomes an explicit economic negotiation over **collateral lockup (`TokenEscrow`) versus breakable credit spread (`CheckCreate`)**, directly creating contract diversity without artificial rules.

## 4. Cardinal Net-Equity Scoring, Private Mandates, and PBRS

### 4.1 Terminal Cardinal Net Equity (Replacing Binary Win/Loss)

Every seat $i$ maximizes continuous **Total Equity** at terminal turn $T \in [24, 32]$:
$$\text{Total Equity}_i(T) = \text{Project Equity}_i(T) + \text{Net Asset Equity}_i(T) + \text{Mandate Bonus}_i(T)$$
- **Project Equity:** Irreversible points earned by consuming Tier-3 Refined bundles at Authority during Turns `1` through $T$.
- **Net Asset Equity ($\Phi_i(T)$) and Final Holding Marks (Director Decision of `2026-11-16`, amended `#54`):** At Season Close, remaining `CRED` and collateralized `TokenEscrow` / `DualEscrowVault` receivables minus unpaid `CRED` auto-debt and open `CheckCreate` liabilities are combined with each seat's leftover commodity units valued by one three-part rule: (1) **trade prices do not count**—no executed trade price, cleared volume, lookback window, or qualification test enters the close mark; (2) **earlier-turn standing bids first (`created_turn < turn` and `>= 1.000` unit depth)**—units that executable non-self resting `Buy` bids that were **already resting when the closing turn began (`o.created_turn < state.turn`)** and cover at least one whole unit (`remaining_units >= UNIT_SCALE = 1_000`) would buy are walked highest-bid-first, adding `min(take * bid_price, locked_cred)` for each slice that strictly beats the unrounded `50%` base-value floor and pooling all other covered slices with the uncovered remainder (bids placed during the closing turn `created_turn >= turn` and sub-unit bids `< UNIT_SCALE` are excluded so neither late unhittable bids nor `0.001`-unit dust bids can lift marks; each holder is valued independently against eligible resting bid depth); and (3) **the rest under one pooled `50%` floor** (`UNSOLD_EXCESS_PARITY_BPS = 5_000`), rounding once over all pooled non-winning and uncovered units. Thus no unit is ever marked below `50%` of base value, and a unit marks above `50%` of base value only where an executable non-self standing bid placed before the closing turn with `>= 1.000` unit above `50%` covers it. Intermediate per-turn PBRS potentials $\Psi_i(s_t)$ retain converter fundamental shadow parities, and exact ties in final `Total Equity` break deterministically by the closing turn's HKDF `"tiebreak"` beacon stream rather than seat index.
- **Private Charter Mandates (`T5` ruling `2026-11-15`, `M3`, and Director Decisions of `2026-11-15` & `2026-11-16`):** Each table publishes a graded mandate deck carrying objective cards worth `3` to `8` mandate points (`3_000..=8_000` milli-points; default difficulty figures remain provisional placeholders pending playtest calibration). Mandate points convert at Season Close per Formula 3 ($\text{round\_half\_up}(m_i \times \alpha \times \max(\text{Med}(W(T)), 0.5\,W_0))$ in integer milli-units), where $\alpha = 0.025$ (`DEFAULT_MANDATE_ALPHA_BPS = 250`, `2.5%` inside the `2%–3%` band), $\text{Med}(W(T))$ is the table-median pre-mandate net worth at close, and $W_0$ (`MANDATE_STARTING_NW_FLOOR_BPS = 5_000` for `0.5 * W_0`) is the table-median starting net worth (with even-$N$ medians defined as the half-up integer midpoint of the two middle seats), retuned from playtests toward mandates explaining roughly `10%–18%` of cross-seat score variance and overturning the pre-mandate net-worth leader in roughly `15%–25%` of games. Before Turn 1 opens, Steward draws fresh 256-bit OS CSPRNG entropy (`deal_seed` and per-seat salts, never derived from any public layout `seed`) to deal one unique card without replacement per seat (`dealt_mandate_commits` in `TableSpec`); mental-poker dealing is deferred to human Game-mode tables (Decision 4, `2026-11-15`). A Turn-1 `submit_mandate_commitment` echo remains optional, and an unrevealed mandate scores `0`.

Because every marginal unit of Equity increases final standing and score, trailing seats retain constructive trading incentives through the final turn: punishing a traitor is priced as rational deterrence rather than zero-cost "dead-player" kingmaking.

### 4.2 Anti-Wash Invariance via Potential-Based Reward Shaping (PBRS)

Neither pure activity proxies (trade count, DEX volume, message count—which trigger ping-pong wash trading) nor purely sparse terminal win/loss rewards suffice for stable training. v2 defines dense intermediate shaping via **Potential-Based Reward Shaping (PBRS)**:
$$F_i(s_t, a_t, s_{t+1}) = \gamma\,\Phi_i(s_{t+1}) - \Phi_i(s_t)$$
where $\Phi_i(s_t)$ is Seat $i$'s fundamental mark-to-market Net Equity at state $s_t$. By Ng, Harada, and Russell (1999), PBRS preserves optimal policy invariance and guarantees every zero-surplus wash cycle ($A \leftrightarrow B$) or self-bidding loop telescopes to $\le 0$ net shaped return (strictly negative once `TransferRate` tariffs and transaction fees apply), while legitimate converter runs and trade surpluses receive immediate dense credit.

## 5. Politics: Tariffs, Cartels, and Rotating Institutional Charters

### 5.1 Quadrennial Council Elections (Every 4 Turns: Turns 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32; `T1–T3` ruling `2026-11-15`, `M1`)

Candidates may file a Phase-1 `StandForOffice` platform specifying proposed export tariffs (`0%` to `35%`) and cartel domain admissions or revocations prior to the vote. Seats vote simultaneously in Phase 2 (weighted by rule-defined franchise—equal seat vote or Project-Equity/credit-gated franchise as parameterized by the chapter pack) to fill two political offices for the next 4-turn term:
1. **Trade Minister (Tariff Authority):** Sets per-commodity export tariffs (`0%` to `35%`) applied on-ledger by Authority via issuer `AccountSet` `TransferRate`. Tariffs tax external settlement flows and transfer tariff incidence between net exporters and importers without blocking trade altogether.
2. **Cartel Chair (Bloc Governance):** Zero seats belong to any cartel and the Cartel Chair office starts vacant (`cartel_chair: None`, consistent with `T1`) at table start; the elected Cartel Chair founds and governs cartel trade blocs implemented on-ledger via `PermissionedDomainSet` (`1` to `10` `AcceptedCredentials`) and `PermissionedDEX` (`OfferCreate` carrying `DomainID`).
   - Within a cartel domain, members trade at reduced or zero bloc tariff surcharges without crossing open-book offers.
   - **Instant Embargo (`CredentialDelete`):** The Cartel Chair (or council majority rule) can revoke a target seat's cartel credential via `CredentialDelete`, immediately invalidating that rival's resting domain offers (`tecNO_PERMISSION`) on subsequent fills.

Under Director rulings `T1–T3` (`2026-11-15`), `2026-11-17`, and engine enforcement (`M1`):
- **Platform enactment (`T3`, `M1`):** A `StandForOffice` platform takes effect when that candidate wins an office with at least `1` vote, even if the winning candidate abstained from casting a Phase-2 ballot (`T3`). Zero-vote elections retain the existing officer indices without enacting any platform, so never-elected starting officers cannot found cartels or change tariffs without winning a vote (`M1`). Stale un-enacted platforms clear at the end of every election turn.
- **One elected office per seat (`2026-11-17`):** At each Turn-`4n` election, Trade Minister is decided first (`b"trade_minister"`), and the Trade Minister winner is excluded from the Cartel Chair count. The Cartel Chair goes to the next candidate with at least `1` vote (breaking ties over remaining candidates with `break_tie_with_turn_beacon`, `b"cartel_chair"`). If no eligible chair candidate has a vote, the chair becomes **vacant** (`None`) when the incumbent chair just became Trade Minister; otherwise `M1` retains the incumbent Cartel Chair (or keeps `None` if already vacant). **This rule limits a seat, not a bloc.**
- **Tie-breaking (`T2` + Director Decision 1 of `2026-11-15`, `#31`, `#29`):** Plurality ties among candidates with the highest positive vote tally are broken deterministically from the turn beacon's domain-separated RFC 5869 `HKDF-SHA256` `"tiebreak"` stream (`expand_turn_stream` keyed by table ID, turn, and office tag). `TableSpec` and `SeasonReplayLog` each record a required execution mode (`TableMode::Game` or `TableMode::Eval`), and `replay_season` refuses any mode mismatch or seed-derived beacon. **Game mode** binds beacons to the pre-named `drand quicknet` round's `SHA-256(drand_signature)` after verifying the BLS12-381 signature offline against the pinned `quicknet` chain public key (`DRAND_QUICKNET_PUBLIC_KEY`), while **Eval mode** binds beacons to the isolated orchestrator's sealed CSPRNG seed via proofs under the RFC 9381 ECVRF construction with C2SP's `ristretto255` ciphersuite (`c2sp.org/vrf-r255`, `vrf-r255 0.1.0`, not registered in RFC 9381; `EvalVrf`), and live Eval engines carrying `eval_vrf_pubkey` reject unverified raw or seeded sweeps.

### 5.2 Rotating Syndicate Charters (Every 6 Turns: Turns 6, 12, 18, 24, 30)

Every 6 turns, seats submit simultaneous sealed equity/`CRED` bids for time-bounded institutional **Syndicate Charters**, issued on-ledger as `Credentials` carrying explicit `Expiration` timestamps (automatically invalidated upon domain checks once expired):
1. **Clearinghouse Broker:** Holds the `tfHybrid` cross-domain bridge credential, enabling `OfferCreate` with `tfHybrid` to arbitrage and bridge liquidity between rival cartel `PermissionedDomains` and the open `CRED` book.
2. **Customs Inspector (§5.2 amended `2026-11-17`):** Receives deterministic audit rights to issue Phase-1 `FlagWashFreeze` against another seat for the turn by posting `wash_freeze_bond_cred` (`DEFAULT_WASH_FREEZE_BOND_CRED = 500`, refused with `InsufficientBalance` if free `CRED` is lower) from free `CRED` into an engine hold (not an escrow). The freeze remains forward-only (no reversal of past fills; blocking the target's DEX offers, Sweep batch fills, swaps, deliveries, and dual escrows for that turn), while Sweep Step 3 evaluates the pure `audit_wash_freeze` predicate over the previous turn's per-turn `FillRecord` log (`fill_log` / `SweepReceipt.fills`): confirmed if **(a)** the target sent and received the same commodity with the same peer `>= wash_round_trip_min_units` each way in that turn, or **(b)** the target bought or sold a peer fill whose `unit_price_cred` exceeds that good's `2.25 × parity` town-import price (`town_import_cost_cred`). On confirmed audit, the Inspector recovers the bond plus `wash_freeze_bounty_cred` collected from the frozen seat's free `CRED` (capped at available free `CRED`); on a clean audit, the whole bond goes to the frozen seat as damages and none of it returns to the Inspector (Publisher ruling `2026-11-17`: returning part of the bond would make a frivolous freeze nearly free; the bond is the one amount, with no separate damages parameter). All three parameters (`wash_round_trip_min_units = 1_000`, `wash_freeze_bond_cred = 500`, `wash_freeze_bounty_cred = 500`) are provisional pending playtest calibration.
3. **Oracle Auditor:** Receives the Turn $t$ private `Horizon Shock` preimage/hint encrypted to its registered 33-byte `AccountSet` `MessageKey` and hash-anchored inside its `Credential` `URI`. The Oracle Auditor can **sell the cryptographically verifiable preimage** for `CRED` or goods, or **bluff with unverified rumors**.

## 6. Horizon Shocks and the 24–32 Turn Season with Random Close

### 6.1 Three-Stage Telegraphed Horizon Shocks (`OracleSet`)

Unannounced shocks add pure lottery variance, while static fixed-length tables solidify into permanent "Blue Donut" non-aggression pacts. v2 uses **3-stage telegraphed crises** anchored on-ledger by Authority via `OracleSet` (`PriceOracle`):
- **Turn $t$ (Private Foresight):** Authority anchors the hidden shock seed hash and delivers the verifiable preimage/hint privately to the current **Oracle Auditor** (encrypted via `MessageKey`).
- **Turn $t+1$ (Narrowed Public Forecast):** Authority publishes a narrowed probabilistic range (commodity sector, direction, bounded magnitude) via `OracleSet` so all seats can hedge via `TokenEscrow`, `CheckCreate`, and inventory positioning.
- **Turn $t+2$ (Realized Outcome):** Authority commits the realized outcome via `OracleSet` and executes the shock deterministically:
  - *Supply Freeze:* temporary trust-line freeze / reduced Turn $t+2$ extraction yield on one $R_k$.
  - *Tariff Holiday:* temporary `TransferRate` reset to `0%` on a targeted commodity.
  - *Demand Surge:* elevated Project Equity multiplier for specific Refined bundles for 2 turns.
  - *Spoilage Spike:* temporary elevation of `Clawback` decay on one raw good.

**Randomness Architecture (Director Decision 1, `2026-11-15`, resolving `#31`):**
- **Game Mode (`TableMode::Game`, `ExternalDrand`, `#29`):** Horizon Shocks, the stochastic closing roll, council plurality tie-breaks, and simultaneous DEX batch tie-breaks all draw from the logged **drand quicknet** beacon (`turn_beacon = SHA-256(drand_signature)`) of the round pre-named in `DrandSchedule` before moves open for that turn (`round = floor((close - 1692803367) / 3) + 1`, never falling back to steerable ledger hashes). Both `TurnEngine::sweep_turn_with_drand` and offline `replay_season` verify each logged 48-byte compressed BLS12-381 G1 signature against the pinned League of Entropy `quicknet` chain public key (`DRAND_QUICKNET_PUBLIC_KEY`, chain hash `52db9ba70e0cc0f6eaf7803dd07447a1f5477735fd3f661792ba94600c84e971`, scheme `bls-unchained-g1-rfc9380`) via `blst` (`Apache-2.0`, NCC Group audited) with `portable` + `no-threads` for cross-platform determinism, returning `DrandBeaconAuthStatus::Authenticated` on `ReplayOutcome.drand_beacon_auth_status` and refusing any Game log carrying pre-seeded shocks, invalid signatures, or beacons/shocks/closing turns that diverge from the verified rounds.
- **Eval Mode (`TableMode::Eval`, `EvalVrf`):** Randomness draws from a fresh 256-bit OS CSPRNG seed (`SealedEvalSeed`, private field, non-serializable) held inside the sealed orchestrator setup isolated from agent sandboxes, never derived from the public layout `seed`. Each turn in `SeasonReplayLog` carries an 80-byte proof (`eval_vrf_proof` verified against `eval_vrf_pubkey`) under the RFC 9381 ECVRF construction instantiated with C2SP's `ristretto255` ciphersuite (`c2sp.org/vrf-r255`, `vrf-r255 0.1.0`, which is not one of the ciphersuites registered in RFC 9381), allowing third-party auditors to verify every turn's randomness draw afterwards without exposing `SealedEvalSeed` or unplayed future turns, while `TurnEngine` also refuses unverified raw or seeded sweeps whenever `eval_vrf_pubkey` is present.
- **Per-Use HKDF Stream Separation:** Neither mode derives randomness from the public layout seed, and every consumer expands its own domain-separated RFC 5869 `HKDF-SHA256` stream (`"shocks"`, `"close_roll"`, `"tiebreak"`, and `"batch_tie"`, bound to `table_id` and `turn`).
- **`league-dice` Retired (`2026-11-17`):** `crates/league-dice` is retired (`2026-11-17`). Turn randomness, `PreimageSecret`, `RevealSalt`, and commit-reveal live in `league-core::commit` (guarded against seed-derived secrets by `crates/league-core/tests/fu1_no_seed_secret.rs`), while the XRPL `PREIMAGE-SHA-256` wire condition/fulfillment encoding and `EscrowFinish` fee rule live separately in `league-ledger::escrow` (the two encodings are not interchangeable).

### 6.2 24–32 Turn Season with Stochastic Closing Roll (Director Decision 3, `2026-11-15`)

Every season runs between **24 and 32 simultaneous turns** (`HARD_TURN_CAP = 32`), preventing both extreme-horizon drift and backward-induction end-game unraveling:
- **Turns 1–23:** Season remains open unconditionally (`CLOSING_ROLL_START_TURN = 24`).
- **Turns 24–31:** At Step 8 of Sweep, the turn's domain-separated HKDF `"close_roll"` stream rolls against the named provisional closing hazard table `DEFAULT_CLOSING_HAZARD_BPS = [3_500; 8]` (`35.00%` per turn across Turns `24..=31`), keeping expected season length near `26` turns (`E[T] = 25.80` turns, with `3.19%` probability of reaching Turn `32`).
- **Turn 32:** Unconditional hard-cap season close. All Turn-1 Private Charter Mandates reveal (or score `0`), final holdings are marked under §4.1's close-marking rule, and terminal Cardinal Net Equity is finalized with beacon tie-breaking.

### 7.4 Layout Seed Pools and Generator-Version Hygiene

Once a policy trains on a chiral table layout or a designer tunes rules against that seed, the seed can no longer serve as an uncontaminated evaluation benchmark. `league-gen` partitions the `u64` layout-seed space into six disjoint, inclusive categories via a single boundary table (`SEED_POOL_TABLE`) and classifier (`seed_pool(seed)`), independent of seat count (`N in {4, 5, 6}`) or `GeneratorConfig`:

| Pool | Layout Seeds (Inclusive) | Intended Role |
|---|---|---|
| `dev` | `0 – 9,999` | Generator gates, code reviews, rule calibration, stubs, and exploratory playtests while rules are still evolving |
| `train` | `10,000 – 999,999` | Training-logged games; `PlrSampler` (`PlrEntry`) accepts and proposes seeds strictly within this range |
| `probe` | `1,000,000 – 1,099,999` | No-gradient forward-pass diagnostic checks during training |
| `held_out` | `2,000,000 – 2,099,999` | Counted evaluation of a frozen trained policy or finalized rule change |
| `sealed` | `2^63 – 2^64−1` | Final check against an out-of-repo 256-seed set; runs only when table purpose is `sealed` |
| `unassigned` | Every other `u64` | Reserved gap space; refused for every table purpose |

- **Checked Setup, Logging & Redaction (`league-gen` & `league-core`):** Game setups call `generate_ring_table_for_setup` (or `generate_ring_table_for_purpose`, defaulting to `purpose = dev`, `training_logged = false`), which verifies `seed_pool(seed)` before invoking `generate_ring_table`, refuses any mismatch or `unassigned` seed with `SEED_POOL_MISMATCH`, refuses `training_logged = true` outside `purpose = train` with `TRAINING_LOGGED_MISMATCH`, and returns `(CheckedRingTable, FeasibilityReport, GameTableRecord)`. For `TablePurpose::Sealed`, `league-gen` (`GameTableRecord` / `RecordedLayoutSeed` and every `GenError` refusal) and `league-core` (`SeasonReplayLog.seed: ReplayLayoutSeed`, `#56`) carry only the pool name (`"sealed"`) and `layout_seed_digest(seed)` (lowercase 64-hex `SHA-256(seed.to_be_bytes())`, shared with `league-gen::sealed_seed_sha256`), never the clear seed, while non-`sealed` replay logs (`< 2^63`) serialize the bare JSON `u64` byte-identically and numeric `sealed`-range values in the clear path are refused with `SEALED_SEED_IN_CLEAR`. In `SeasonReplayLog::new_sealed` / `from_rig_seed`, the rig checks `deal_seed` and Eval-VRF independence against the clear seed before redacting and records the log writer's unverified claim `independence_checked_at_write_unverified` (not replay-verified off-rig without the seed). On the generator side, `CheckedRingTable::Sealed(SealedRingTable)` omits `Serialize`, reduces `seed` to pool+digest in `Debug`, and exposes `RingTable` only via `unseal_table_with_clear_seed` / `into_unsealed_table_with_clear_seed`. What remains the future table bootstrap and `#58` work: never formatting, logging, or serializing an unsealed `RingTable`, rejecting `is_reserved_test_seed(seed)` when scheduling real games, and replacing the unkeyed 8-byte digest plus unverified writer independence claim with `#58`'s rig-keyed seed commitment before any real `sealed` record leaves the rig.
- **Benchmark & Version Separation:**
  - A `held_out` seed is **never** used to choose or tune a model checkpoint, prompt, or game rule; selection decisions use `dev` or `probe` runs, and `held_out` scores an already-frozen candidate.
  - Results produced under different `GENERATOR_VERSION` strings are **never** pooled into one combined evaluation or training summary.
- **Rule for Tests and Gates:**
  - Unit tests, property sweeps, and generator gates call raw `generate_ring_table` with `dev`-pool seeds (`0 – 9,999`) only.
  - A test may use `train`-pool seeds (`10,000 – 999,999`) only when exercising `train`-pool logic (such as `PlrSampler`) or where an existing test already did so prior to seed-pool enforcement.
  - Seeds in `probe`, `held_out`, and the `sealed` range never appear in tests **except the reserved test values in the table below** (plus non-generating `seed_pool` / refusal checks), and the future table bootstrap must call `is_reserved_test_seed` and never schedule a reserved value for a real game; actual out-of-repo sealed seeds are never placed in code, tests, logs, or docs.
- **Documented Exceptions, Reserved Test Values, and Pre-Existing `train`-Range Test Seeds:**
